Our Story

Our Heritage

Established in 1978, Nundy's introduces a whole range of its products which speaks of style quotients and class. The company offers "the new style-conscious buyers" an entire range of modern furniture & interior for offices, ships, school, college and hospitals. The company has achieved a growing recognition in the national market by manufacturing world class products in matching designs and patterns. The manufacturing unit of the company is outfitted with latest machines and equipments. We constantly make an effort to create high-quality furniture that provides intelligent functions and unrivalled convenience for stylish living and the work environment. With its superlative quality of products, the company has already furnished more than 150 offices, over 50 Schools, Colleges & Management institutions across 15 Mn Sq Feet and over 100 Repeat customers.

Dola Mallik

Dola Mallik, the visionary Managing Partner of Nundy's, has been steering the family business since 1980 with over two decades of expertise in crafting exceptional interior solutions. Established in 1978, Nundy's, under Dola's leadership, has become a trusted name in delivering stylish, high-quality furniture and interiors for offices, schools, colleges, hospitals, and even Indian Navy vessels. With a focus on creating environments that invigorate, delight, and add value, Dola has driven Nundy's to furnish over 150 offices, 50+ educational institutions across 15 million square feet, and earned the loyalty of over 100 repeat customers.

Dola's approach blends innovation with practicality, ensuring tailor-made solutions that meet diverse client needs while respecting financial constraints. From bare shells to warm, ergonomic fit-outs, Nundy's thrives on transforming spaces with cutting-edge designs that are both elegant and future-proof. No project is too big or too small for Dola and her team, who are committed to delivering unmatched quality and convenience, backed by a state-of-the-art manufacturing unit equipped with the latest machinery.

With a passion for excellence and a customer-centric ethos, Dola Mallik continues to redefine interior solutions, making Nundy's a beacon of style, functionality, and reliability in the national market. Ready to elevate your space? Dola and Nundy's are just a call away, poised to deliver bespoke solutions for all your interior needs.
